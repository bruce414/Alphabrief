# Premium Differentiation: Environment-Aware Analysis

## Overview

The free tier focuses primarily on the financial entity mentioned in the submitted source. For example, if a user submits an article about Apple, Alphabrief will summarise the source and provide company-level context such as Apple’s recent earnings, business performance, key financial signals, and company-specific risks.

The premium tier expands the analysis beyond the individual company. It provides environment-aware investment insights by analysing the company within its broader industry, competitive, macroeconomic, political, regulatory, and technological context.

This makes the premium tier meaningfully different from the free tier. Free users receive a company-focused brief, while premium users receive a deeper 360° investor brief.

---

## Free Tier: Company-Level Brief

The free tier should answer:

> What does this source mean for the company mentioned?

Included sections:

- Source summary
- Key takeaways
- Company overview
- Recent earnings and performance
- Key numbers mentioned
- Company-specific bull case
- Company-specific bear case
- Company-specific risks
- Final takeaway

---

## Premium Tier: 360° Environment-Aware Brief

The premium tier should answer:

> What does this source mean for the company within the broader environment it operates in?

Included sections:

- Everything included in the free tier
- Industry context
- Competitor comparison
- Macroeconomic factors
- Political and regulatory factors
- Supply chain factors
- Consumer demand factors
- Interest rate and currency sensitivity
- Technology disruption risks
- Broader catalysts
- Scenario analysis
- Final investor view

---

## Example

If a user submits an article about Apple’s iPhone sales:

The free tier focuses on Apple’s iPhone performance, earnings impact, business segment exposure, and company-specific risks.

The premium tier additionally analyses smartphone industry maturity, competition from Samsung and Huawei, China market exposure, consumer spending conditions, regulatory pressure, supply chain risk, and Apple’s broader services and AI strategy.

---

## Product Principle

Free tier analysis should be company-centred.

Premium tier analysis should be environment-aware.

This creates a clear value gap between free and premium without making the free tier useless.
